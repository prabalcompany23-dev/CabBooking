# Business case — Book-a-Ride Phase 1

**Source:** [BRD Word doc](../BRD_Book-a-Ride_Phase1_Phase2.docx)

## Executive summary

Add transport after venue booking on Playo. Phase 1 validates demand with manual ops (low engineering risk); Phase 2 automation is gated on pilot data.

## Recommended option

Proceed with **Phase 1 manual pilot** (single city/sport) before any ride-partner integration.

## Benefits (Phase 1 focus)

- Closes user journey gap (getting to venue)  
- Measures real ride demand  
- Avoids premature payment/partner investment  

## Success metrics (Phase 1)

- Requests received and fulfilled in pilot  
- Request rate vs eligible bookings  

## Risks (Phase 1)

- Low booking frequency · manual ops load · liability of manual coordination
